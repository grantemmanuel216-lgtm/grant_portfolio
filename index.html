A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Technology started as curiosity.
It became a direction.

I have always been interested in what happens behind the things we use every day. At first, I simply enjoyed experimenting and seeing what I could create. Over time, that curiosity turned into a genuine interest in how websites, applications and games are designed and built.

I am currently developing my foundation in software engineering, with a particular long-term interest in game design and interactive experiences. I enjoy learning by building, testing ideas, making mistakes and improving the result.

✳

What drives me: understanding how things work, then learning how to create them myself.

THE STORY SO FAR

My learning journey.

01
2019THE FIRST WEBSITE

My first experience with web creation

My journey into building for the web began in 2019, when I created my first website using Wix. I was working with its drag-and-drop tools, arranging sections, images and text to turn an idea into an actual webpage. I did not understand all the code behind it yet, but seeing something I had put together come to life on a screen made a lasting impression.

Around that time, I also took part in WAAW foundation technology competition and emerged as a winner. That experience encouraged me to take my interest in technology more seriously and showed me that curiosity and creativity could lead to meaningful opportunities.

02
2021GAME MODDING

Exploring how games are put together

By 2021, my curiosity had shifted towards games. I became especially interested in how game characters, environments and visual details were created. I started experimenting with PPSSPP game files, mainly by changing character textures and importing custom skins of my own.

It was an early, hands-on way of learning. I was not building a game engine or writing a full game from scratch; I was modifying visual assets and learning through trial and error. Still, the process made me more curious about the design and technical work behind interactive experiences.

03
Late 2023-2026FORMAL TRAINING

Taking software development seriously

As my interest grew, I began looking beyond the tools I had already used. I wanted to understand programming itself and build the skills needed to create projects independently. This was part of what led me to Afrihub ICT Institute in late 2023, I then got a national innovation diploma in 2026(NID) in software engineering whereby i got direct entry into IFATOSS university at benin republic, my current study location.

During my training, I completed learning programmes in JavaScript and Web Development, earning certificates in both areas. The experience helped me strengthen my technical foundation and gave me a clearer path for continuing to learn, practise and build. then in then in early 2026 i became an intern in intel-box office solutions and worked on 2 mini projects during my 3 month duration, One memorable aspect of the training programme was the weekly project resentation sessions where interns were encouraged to demonstrate their work and explain their development process. During one of these presentations, I showcased an Intern Matching Platform project which received positive feedback and recognition from the Human Resources Manager, Mr. Faisal. Although the project was later discontinued, the experience improved my presentation skills, confidence, and ability to communicate technical concepts effectively.

04
THE NEXT CHAPTERIN PROGRESS

Growing into the developer I want to become

Today, I am continuing to improve my understanding of software development through practice and personal projects. My current focus is on strengthening my web development skills while learning more about programming, problem solving and the creative process behind digital products.

In the long term, I hope to work towards becoming a game designer it has alwaays been my passion. I see software engineering as an important foundation for that goal: it will help me understand not only how a game looks and feels, but also how the systems behind it work.
